## 🚀 What we build at ADIN

ADIN is an AI-driven marketing intelligence platform. Brands plug in their ad accounts;
ADIN unifies the data, watches every campaign and tells teams what to do next.

- **AI Suggestions**: creative, audience, bidding, budget and structure recommendations, campaign by campaign
- **AI Budget Optimizer**: reallocates media budget while the plan is in flight
- **Performance Planner**: splits a budget or KPI target across campaigns before launch
- **Health checks, pacing and alerts**: problems surface before the monthly report does
- **ADIN MCP**: the whole platform, available to Claude and other AI assistants

## 🧠 Capabilities

| | |
|---|---|
| **AI & agents** | LLM agents in production, MCP servers, tool-using assistants for analysts and engineers |
| **Data platforms** | Ingestion from Meta, Google, LinkedIn, CM360, Adform and more; ClickHouse at scale; ETL orchestration |
| **Cloud architecture** | AWS serverless and containers (Lambda, ECS, EventBridge, S3, RDS), built cost-aware |
| **Marketing tech** | Ad platform APIs, cross-channel measurement, budget optimization |
| **Leadership** | Building and running engineering teams, from roadmap to production |
